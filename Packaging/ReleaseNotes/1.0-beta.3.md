### What's in Beta 3
Joining a server now works the way Discord does it, forms from apps and bots are rebuilt, and channels open faster. Plus a long list of fixes. Thank you for every report.

### Fixed
- **Servers that showed no channels at all.** In servers that hide every channel until you have a role, Wingcord could miss your own membership and show an empty list. It now asks Discord for it whenever it's missing.
- **Blurry, zoomed or cut-off pictures in cards and embeds.** Discord changed how its picture service sizes images. Wingcord now asks for every picture in its own shape, so cards, galleries and thumbnails are sharp and whole again, and the viewer opens big pictures sharp.
- **Profile pictures missing until you scrolled.** Opening a channel now shows every picture straight away.
- **Pictures that didn't move on hover.** In bubbles, pointing at any message plays its sender's picture and decoration. The DM list shows decorations again, and they play under the pointer.
- **Activity cards without their cover.** A game's card shows its cover and store button the first time you open it.
- **Reactions with a Nitro Emoji Pack emoji without Nitro.** Pack emoji are locked for reactions and status replies unless you have Nitro, as in Discord. Fake Nitro still works for messages.
- **"0 questions about what you're here for."** That screen is gone (see below).

### Joining servers
- **Questions only when the server has them.** Wingcord shows a server's onboarding only where Discord would, starting on the first question, with the server's rules as the last step when it has both.
- **The rules in Discord's window.** A server that only asks you to agree to its rules shows them in Discord's own window, with the server's card beside them.
- **The message box tells you why you can't talk yet.** "You must complete a few more steps before you can talk", with a Complete button; an application awaiting review, with Withdraw; a rejected one; a server that wants a verified e-mail, with Resend; and the waits for new members and new accounts, with a countdown.
- **A redesigned questions page.** Solid and easy to read over the server's picture, with the server's name and your progress at the top.

### Forms
Forms from apps and bots now open in the same card as Wingcord's own forms.
- **Dropdowns** open as a list instead of spilling every option onto the page.
- **People, roles and channels** can be picked with search.
- **Files** can be added by dropping them in or choosing them.

### Faster
- **Channels open faster.** When the pointer rests on a conversation, Wingcord starts loading it, so it's usually there by the time you click.
- **Member lists you go back to are ready.** Wingcord keeps your last five member lists in a server up to date, as Discord does.
