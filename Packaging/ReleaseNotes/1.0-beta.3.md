### What's in Beta 3
Joining a server now works the way Discord does it, Discover gets a Games tab, the Server Guide keeps track of your first steps, thread cards show their newest message, files get a new card, the quick switcher is redesigned, forms from apps and bots are rebuilt, and channels open faster. Plus a long list of fixes. Thank you for every report.

### Fixed
- **Servers that showed no channels at all.** Wingcord now reads your roles in every server as soon as it connects, so channels only your roles can see are there from the start.
- **"Reconnecting…" with member lists stuck loading.** Wingcord sometimes asked Discord for missing member list rows in a way Discord couldn't read, and Discord dropped the connection. It doesn't any more.
- **Member lists that stayed grey when you went back to a channel.** They fill straight away now, without scrolling.
- **Code blocks that showed up empty.** One-line code blocks, blocks that start in the middle of a sentence and `>>>` quotes now read exactly as they do in Discord, and code wraps at spaces.
- **Older pictures shown as files.** Pictures, videos and sounds from before Discord labelled file types are recognised by their names again.
- **Blurry, zoomed or cut-off pictures in cards and embeds.** Discord changed how its picture service sizes images. Wingcord now asks for every picture in its own shape, so cards, galleries and thumbnails are sharp and whole again.
- **Game covers that looked zoomed in**, like GTA V's. A cover that isn't the usual shape is now shown whole.
- **Profile pictures missing until you scrolled**, and **pictures that didn't move on hover**: in bubbles, pointing at any message plays its sender's picture and decoration, and the DM list shows decorations again.
- **Tapbacks with ten or more** showed "…" instead of the number.
- **Servers opening on a voice channel.** A server you open for the first time lands on its first text channel, as in Discord.
- **Games that wouldn't open their game profile**, PlayStation and Xbox games among them. Clicking a game's name or its cover art in a profile now opens its game profile, as in Discord.
- **A server's name cut off** when you pointed at it in the server list with Discover open, and **a dark bar over the sidebar** on Discover and Channels & Roles.
- **The server tag card** said "The server has limited who can see this profile" twice.
- **Activity cards without their cover**, and **Nitro Emoji Pack reactions without Nitro**.

### Joining servers
- **Questions only when the server has them**, starting on the first question, with the server's rules as the last step when it has both. The "0 questions" screen is gone.
- **The rules in Discord's window**, with channels, roles, emoji and links in them shown as they are in messages.
- **The message box tells you why you can't talk yet**: a few more steps to complete, an application in review or rejected, an e-mail to verify, or a wait for new members and new accounts, with a countdown. A verified phone skips the waits, as in Discord.
- **Never stuck in a channel you can't see.** If the channel you're in becomes hidden, Wingcord moves you to the first one you can see.

### Server Guide
- **Get Started keeps track.** In a server you joined this week, "Get Started · 3 of 5" sits at the top of the channel list, each step gets a check once it's done, and the next step waits above the message box. Visiting a channel or saying something there does a step, as in Discord, and finishing them all gets a little celebration.
- **The server's own pictures** on its steps and resources, like Rockstar's R★, instead of a plain "#".

### Games in Discover
- **A Games tab**: trending games, the games you played lately with their latest update, games like the one you played most recently, and what your friends are playing.
- **Trending**: the week's top games, how much each grew, and their official servers with a Join button.
- **Search games** by name, with platform and genre filters.

### Files
- **A new card for files**: the file's own icon, its kind and size, and one button that downloads it, shows the progress, and then shows it in the Finder.
- **Click a file to Quick Look it**, drag it out to the Finder or Mail, or right-click for Open, Save As… and Copy Link. Uploads show the same progress ring.

### Also new
- **Thread cards show the newest message**: who wrote it, what it says and when, with Discord's message count.
- **Follow announcement channels** into a channel of your own server, from the bar under one.
- **A redesigned quick switcher (⌘K)**, centred in the window, with each server's picture beside its channels and the shortcuts named at the bottom.
- **Profile pictures and banners open full size** from a full profile, flying out of the profile and back.
- **Every server remembers the channel you were in**, even after you quit.
- **Forms from apps and bots** open in the same card as Wingcord's own, with dropdowns, people and channel pickers, and file uploads.
- **Channels open faster**: Wingcord starts loading one when your pointer rests on it, and keeps your last five member lists in a server up to date.
- **The Badge Directory** shows your progress to the next level on every badge that has levels, and a button to wherever each badge is earned.
- **The Void**, in Settings ▸ Experiments: for accounts Discord has let in, throw Orbs into it to level up its badge.
