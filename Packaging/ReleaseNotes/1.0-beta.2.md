### What's in Beta 2
Mostly fixes for what you reported, and a few small additions. Thank you for every report.

### Fixed
- **Pictures that stopped loading.** If you switched pages while the channel list was still scrolling, avatars, nameplates and attachments in conversations and the member list stopped loading for the rest of the session. They load again, and a picture that failed while your connection dropped is fetched again once it's back.
- **An empty or slow member list** after a dropped connection. Wingcord now asks Discord for it again when it reconnects.
- **Channels missing in servers with onboarding.** Wingcord showed only the channels picked during onboarding, even with Show All Channels on. It now follows Discord's rule: channels are hidden only when you turn Show All Channels off. Channels you hid also stop lighting up the server's dot.
- **Calls that rang again.** Starting a call in a conversation whose call was already running rang everyone a second time. Wingcord now joins without ringing, from every place you can start a call.
- **Server roles on a DM profile.** A profile opened from a DM showed roles from the last server you had open.
- **The server name cut to one letter** in a narrow sidebar. When space runs short, the sidebar button and the + menu fold into one, and the server's icon steps aside before the name does. In full screen the name also gets the space of the hidden window buttons.
- **Server icons in the Servers list** showed the corners of a dark tile behind them. They're rounded squares now, with even spacing around the list.
- **Animated server icons** could vanish from the server rail after you hovered them. Animated nameplates had the same problem.
- **Slide to Join** stops at the end of its track and settles without bouncing past it.
- **The viewer's "Couldn't load" error.** The viewer now falls back to the original file, and the error has a Try Again button.
- **A blurry picture in the viewer**, mostly after zooming in quickly or on a large display. It now loads a sharp version sized for your screen.
- **The last people in the member list**, usually the Offline group, only showed while you scrolled past the end.
- **Sort on the Members page** no longer breaks onto two lines.

### Forwarded messages
They work like any other message now. You can select and copy their text, open their pictures and videos in the viewer, reveal their spoilers and see their stickers. The line under a forward says where it came from and when: click it to go to the original message, or to preview its server if you're not in it.

### New
- **Read All.** While any server has unread messages, a Read All button sits above your servers, in the rail and on the Servers page. One click marks every server as read. It's also in the menu bar: Channel ▸ Mark All Servers as Read.
- **Channel spacing.** Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Channel list has two sliders, Space between channels and Space above categories, with a preview.
- **Active Now.** In a wide window, the Friends page shows what your friends are doing: the game they're playing, the music they're listening to, or the voice channel they're in.
- **Server Hub, as an experiment.** Turn it on in Settings ▸ Experiments. Where Discord has given a server a Server Hub, it opens from the top of the channel list: the server's banner, a weekly gaming leaderboard, popular games and music, picture and text cards, and the whiteboard, which runs right on the page. If you can manage the server, you can edit it too: drag widgets between the two columns, add or remove them, change the banner, then save and publish.
- **More Active Posts.** Rest the pointer on a forum in the channel list to see its most active posts, and open one from there. A channel with active threads you don't follow shows them the same way.
- **Echo in calls.** If echo cancellation isn't working on your Mac, the call card says so and suggests headphones.
- **The floating sidebar** sits evenly inside the window, its corners following the window's.

### Good to know
- **After this update, macOS asks once for your Mac's password** so Wingcord can read your saved sign-in. Enter it and click **Always Allow**.

### Found something?
- **Help ▸ Report a Bug…**, or type `/bug` in any conversation. Reports now also say how long channels took to load and why a picture failed, which helps with slow loading.
- **Help ▸ Suggest a Feature…**, or `/idea`.
