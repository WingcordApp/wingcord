### What's in Beta 2
**Vice City.** Discord's limited-time Vice City color theme is in Settings ▸ Appearance while Discord offers it, and Wingcord's app icon wears the look until the campaign ends. The Vice City font is in Display Name Style, free for everyone while the campaign runs.

Beyond that: fixes for what you reported, activities and calls rebuilt, and a lot of smaller additions. Thank you for every report.

### Fixed
- **Pictures that stopped loading.** If you switched pages while the channel list was still scrolling, avatars, nameplates and attachments in conversations and the member list stopped loading for the rest of the session. They load again, and a picture that failed while your connection dropped is fetched again once it's back.
- **An empty or slow member list** after a dropped connection. Wingcord now asks Discord for it again when it reconnects.
- **Channels missing in servers with onboarding.** Wingcord showed only the channels picked during onboarding, even with Show All Channels on. It now follows Discord's rule: channels are hidden only when you turn Show All Channels off. Channels you hid also stop lighting up the server's dot.
- **Calls that rang again.** Starting a call in a conversation whose call was already running rang everyone a second time. Wingcord now joins without ringing, from every place you can start a call.
- **Server roles on a DM profile.** A profile opened from a DM showed roles from the last server you had open.
- **The server name cut to one letter** in a narrow sidebar. When space runs short, the sidebar button and the + menu fold into one, and the server's icon steps aside before the name does. In full screen the name also gets the space of the hidden window buttons.
- **Server icons in the Servers list** showed the corners of a dark tile behind them. They're rounded squares now, with even spacing around the list.
- **Animated server icons** could vanish from the server rail after you hovered them. Animated nameplates had the same problem.
- **Slide to Join** stops at the end of its track and settles without bouncing past it.
- **The viewer's "Couldn't load" error.** The viewer now falls back to the original file, and the error has a Try Again button.
- **A blurry picture in the viewer**, mostly after zooming in quickly or on a large display. It now loads a sharp version sized for your screen.
- **The last people in the member list**, usually the Offline group, only showed while you scrolled past the end.
- **Sort on the Members page** no longer breaks onto two lines.
- **A crash when turning on your camera** from the capture bar.
- **A reaction lifted the sender's name.** The name stays where it is, and the reactions sit beside it.
- **Long text over a smaller picture** in a link's card. A card with a large picture is now as wide as the picture, and its text wraps to fit, as in Discord.
- **Messages Wingcord didn't know** showed as a bare "Message" line. Accepted friend requests, voice hangouts, raid alerts and Server Discovery notices now read as they do in Discord, and a call says how long it lasted, in red if you missed it.
- **Emoji names** follow Discord's: built-in emoji come first, and a server emoji with the same name becomes `:name~1:`.
- **Invites and other cards** didn't draw in the Inbox, search results and pins.
- **Text and icons looked washed out**, especially over a theme. They're brighter and fully opaque now.

### Forwarded messages
They work like any other message now. You can select and copy their text, open their pictures and videos in the viewer, reveal their spoilers and see their stickers. The line under a forward says where it came from and when: click it to go to the original message, or to preview its server if you're not in it.

### Activities
Activities were rebuilt. Joining one that's already running and starting a new one both work reliably now. In a call, an activity is a tile you can join; it floats in a corner when you look elsewhere, and it can pop out into its own window. In a conversation without a call it sits above the messages, and you can drag it taller or shorter. The launcher shows the games you've played and Discord's featured games and collections, and Activity cards in the member list open as you hover, with Join, Listen Along or Watch Stream.

### Calls
- **The call's controls** are one floating capsule over the tiles, shown while the pointer is over the call, and the tiles get the whole height.
- **Video Call** sits beside Call in the toolbar.
- **A call that's already going** shows after you restart Wingcord, and calls reconnect after a network change or when your Mac wakes.
- **A connection panel** shows ping and packet loss in each direction.
- **Streams and cameras** float in a corner when you leave the call's view.

### New
- **Buttons and cards like Discord's.** Buttons on messages from bots and apps are bigger and use Discord's colors for each kind of button, blurple, green, red or grey, whatever your theme or accent color. Link cards, bot cards, invites and events keep Discord's colors in every theme, and the pictures in them open in the viewer.
- **A message box that makes room.** When you reply, edit, schedule a message or add files, the message box grows to hold them, the conversation moving up with it, and shrinks back when you're done. A reply also shows the person's profile picture.
- **Send when there's something to send.** The Send button appears as you start typing, and the buttons beside it slide over to make room.
- **Silent messages.** Start a message with `@silent` to send it without notifications, as in Discord.
- **Nitro Emoji Packs.** With Nitro, Discord's emoji packs are in the emoji picker.
- **Profile pictures that follow long messages.** As you scroll past a long run of messages from one person, their picture moves along beside it and stops at the message above.
- **Frosted bubbles** over a color theme too, not only over a wallpaper.
- **Read All.** While any server has unread messages, a Read All button sits above your servers, in the rail and on the Servers page. One click marks every server as read, forums included, and it shows its progress while it runs. It's also in the menu bar: Channel ▸ Mark All Servers as Read.
- **Channel spacing.** Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Channel list has two sliders, Space between channels and Space above categories, with a preview.
- **Pin Channel to Top.** Right-click a channel to keep it at the top of its list.
- **Active Now.** In a wide window, the Friends page shows what your friends are doing: the game they're playing, the music they're listening to, or the voice channel they're in.
- **Server Hub.** Where Discord has given a server a Server Hub, it opens from the top of the channel list: the server's banner, a weekly gaming leaderboard, popular games and music, picture and text cards, and the whiteboard, which runs right on the page. If you can manage the server, you can edit it too: drag widgets between the two columns, add or remove them, change the banner, then save and publish. Wingcord follows Discord's own rollout, and you can turn it off in Settings ▸ Experiments.
- **The Server Guide** is a welcome page that fills the window, and the channels in it open beside it.
- **More Active Posts.** Rest the pointer on a forum in the channel list to see its most active posts, and open one from there. A channel with active threads you don't follow shows them the same way.
- **The viewer** plays videos with Apple's player, keeps a small player in the chat, and has a cinema mode and Live Text. To zoom, pinch, scroll or double-click.
- **Echo in calls.** If echo cancellation isn't working on your Mac, the call card says so and suggests headphones.
- **The floating sidebar** sits evenly inside the window, its corners following the window's.
- **And a lot of other fixes and improvements.**

### Good to know
- **After this update, macOS asks once for your Mac's password** so Wingcord can read your saved sign-in. Enter it and click **Always Allow**.
- **Message previews are off by default now.** Each one is a request to Discord for every conversation in your list. Turn them back on in Settings ▸ Appearance.

### Found something?
- **Help ▸ Report a Bug…**, or type `/bug` in any conversation. Reports now also say how long channels took to load and why a picture failed, which helps with slow loading.
- **Help ▸ Suggest a Feature…**, or `/idea`.
