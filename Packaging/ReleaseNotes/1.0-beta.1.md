### Welcome to the first beta
Wingcord is a native Discord client for the Mac, made for macOS 27. Thank you for trying it this early.

### Good to know
- **Opening it the first time:** double-click Wingcord. macOS won't open it, because it doesn't come from the App Store or a registered developer: click **Done**, not Move to Trash. Then open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** beside Wingcord and enter your password. You only do this once.
- **Updates come by themselves.** Wingcord checks every few hours, downloads a new version in the background and installs it when you quit. When one is ready, a green arrow appears at the end of the toolbar: click it to restart into it now.
- **After each update, macOS asks once for your Mac's password** to let Wingcord read your saved sign-in: *Wingcord wants to access key “Wingcord sign-in” in your keychain.* Enter it and click **Always Allow**. The question is macOS's own, and the password stays with macOS. Signed in to more than one account? It asks once for each.
- **Keep Wingcord in Applications.** It can't update itself from its disk image or from Downloads, and it offers to move itself if it finds itself there.
